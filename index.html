FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about working with MissWeekes Management? Find quick answers to common queries here.

How do artist bookings work?

When a client requests a booking, we handle the communications, coordinate calendar availability, negotiate contract rates and performance riders, draft technical agreements, and collect deposits. This keeps the booking process secure and efficient for both artists and venues.

Can you manage my social media?

Yes. We offer monthly packages that cover creative copywriting, hashtag planning, post scheduling, engagement outreach, and performance reporting for Instagram, TikTok, and other visual networks.

Do you work with brands?

Absolutely. We build links between lifestyle brands and creative talents. We coordinate sponsored projects, organic alignment placements, event sponsorships, and co-branded merchandise activations.

Can restaurants and venues hire you?

Yes. We provide digital marketing launches, influencer activations, ticket promotion strategies, and curation of DJ/live acts for high-end dining and cocktail spaces in Birmingham and across the UK.

Do you organise events?

Yes, we manage logistics, vendor booking, promotional planning, and operations direction for showcases, venue launches, and VIP events.

Do you help new artists?

Yes. Our Talent Development service is designed to help emerging musicians and DJs establish their brand. We consult on press kits, social media structure, visual design, and performance booking readiness.

Do you offer consultations?

Yes. We offer paid, one-on-one discovery and advisory consultation calls where we review your current brand assets, discuss your goals, and outline actionable steps you can implement.

How do collaborations work?

We start with a discovery form or consultation call. If there is mutual alignment, we propose a strategic arrangement (e.g., campaign booking, brand deal, or long-term management framework) and proceed with contracts.
